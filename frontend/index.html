PHYSICS · KINEMATICS

Uniform Motion & Velocity

1. Displacement

Displacement is the change in position: final position minus initial position. It has direction, so its sign depends on the coordinate system.

Δx = xf − xi

2. Average velocity

Average velocity is displacement divided by elapsed time. It is not always equal to average speed.

vavg = Δx / Δt
Quick check: distance is the total path length; displacement depends only on initial and final positions.

Average acceleration

Velocity changes from 4 m/s to 16 m/s in 3 s. Find average acceleration.

Your answer: 6 m/s²

Correct answer: 4 m/s²

Solution: a = (16 − 4) / 3 = 4 m/s².

Common mistake: dividing final velocity by time instead of change in velocity.
